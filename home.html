OUR STORY

It took twelve years, two high school diplomas, two college degrees, and a few state borders to realize what everyone else already knew: we were meant to be.

Though colleges and careers scattered us across different zip codes, our friendship never wavered.

Somewhere between shared memories and growing into adulthood, we realized our favorite person was right beside us the entire time, and we couldn’t be more thrilled for this next chapter.

How did you two meet?

Rajat: We met the summer before high school! Our high school does a summer program where students can take a class before their freshmen year starts, and I used to go to her classroom before class started.

Who made the first move? How did it happen?

Disha: I invited him to be my plus one to my company’s holiday party and it turned into a date!

What’s your most memorable date?

Disha: We took a day trip upstate to a town along the river and spent the day bopping through little boutiques, finding cute places to eat and take photos, and people watching.

What’s your favorite thing to do together?

Rajat: We love checking out fun looking stores and new cafés!

Where are you going for your honeymoon?

Disha: Anywhere with beautiful beaches! Maybe the Maldives—while it’s still above water?

What’s the best meal you’ve shared together?

Rajat: Showing up to Chor Bazaar in Manhattan right as they open to eat some dahi puri

JAIPUR TRAVEL

Flying In

For those a flight away, we recommend flying into either Jaipur International Airport (JAI) or Indira Gandhi International Airport, New Delhi (DEL).

From Jaipur International Airport

You can easily book an app-based cab (Uber or Ola) or hire a prepaid taxi directly from the arrivals terminal.

Distance & Drive Time: ~45 km (approx. 1 hour)

Estimated Fare: ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 INR.

Jaipur International Airport (JAI), Airport Road, Sanganer, Jaipur, Rajasthan, India

From Indira Gandhi International Airport

We recommend hiring a dedicated outstation cab directly from Delhi Airport to Chomu Palace.

Distance & Drive Time: ~250 km (approx. 5 hours)

Estimated Fare: ₹4,000 to ₹6,000 INR per vehicle (one-way).

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), New Delhi, Delhi, India

Driving Yourself or Bringing a Personal Driver?

Valet Parking: Complimentary valet parking will be available at the palace throughout all wedding events.

Driver Lodging & Meals: If you are traveling with a private driver, we are happy to arrange accommodation and meals for them. Please provide advance notice by Feb 1st through your RSVP form.

Quick Tips for Out-of-Town & International Guests

Visas: International guests should apply for an Indian e-Tourist Visa online at least 3 to 4 weeks before traveling.

Connectivity: We recommend activating an international roaming plan, purchasing an eSIM before departure (e.g., Airalo), or getting a local SIM card at the airport arrivals hall (Airtel inside Delhi airport).

Payments: Credit cards are widely accepted, but we recommend keeping ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 in cash handy for highway tolls, driver tips, and small roadside stops.
